About

A little about me

I am interested in understanding algebraic structures through examples, computations, and the patterns hiding behind them.

My usual research strategy: compute a few examples, guess something unreasonable, and then try very hard to prove it.

I am currently a Sabarmati Bridge Fellow at the Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar. My recent work involves rational points over finite fields, Danielewski-type varieties, and questions connected with the Zariski Cancellation Problem.

Alongside this work, I am developing my research interests in finite fields and their applications, particularly where they interact with algebraic coding theory and computational algebra.

Research

Research interests

The main themes I currently think about.

Finite Fields and Applications

Polynomial arithmetic, irreducibility, primitive elements, factorization, rational points, and algebraic applications of finite fields.

Algebraic Coding Theory

Codes over finite fields, covering problems, algebraic constructions, and structural questions arising in coding theory.

Commutative Algebra

Affine domains, polynomial rings, filtrations, exponential maps, and related invariants.

Affine Algebraic Geometry

Danielewski-type varieties, Makar-Limanov invariants, and questions related to cancellation.

Computational Algebra

Symbolic and experimental mathematics using SageMath, Macaulay2, Python, and related tools.
