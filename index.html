I am currently a Sabarmati Bridge Fellow at the Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar. My interests lie broadly in algebra, finite fields and their applications.

My recent work concerns rational points over finite fields, Danielewski-type varieties, and questions related to the Zariski Cancellation Problem. I am also interested in developing stronger connections with algebraic coding theory.

Research Interests

Some of the mathematical areas and problems I am currently studying.

Finite Fields

Polynomial constructions, arithmetic over finite fields, primitive elements, factorization, and rational-point counting.

Algebraic Coding Theory

Finite-field methods, algebraic codes, covering questions, and related structural problems.

Commutative Algebra

Affine domains, polynomial rings, exponential maps, invariants, and cancellation phenomena.

Affine Algebraic Geometry

Danielewski-type varieties, Makar-Limanov invariants, and the Zariski Cancellation Problem.

Rational Point Counting

Counting rational points on algebraic varieties over finite fields.

Current Explorations

Polygonal-number generating varieties, generalized covering radius, and computational algebra.
