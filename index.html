About

I am currently a Sabarmati Bridge Fellow at the Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar, where I continue my research in mathematics.

My interests lie broadly in algebra, with particular interest in finite fields and their applications, algebraic coding theory, commutative algebra, and algebraic geometry.

I completed my M.Sc. in Mathematics at IIT Gandhinagar. My research has involved rational points over finite fields, Danielewski-type varieties, and questions connected with the Zariski Cancellation Problem.

Research

My research interests lie at the intersection of algebra, finite fields, and arithmetic geometry.

Finite Fields

Polynomial constructions over finite fields, rational point counting, and combinatorial and arithmetic structures over finite fields.

Algebraic Coding Theory

Algebraic and finite-field methods arising in coding theory, with particular interest in the structural mathematics underlying codes.

Commutative Algebra

Affine algebras, polynomial rings, locally nilpotent derivations, exponential maps, and cancellation-type problems.

Algebraic Geometry

Affine varieties over finite fields, rational points, Danielewski-type varieties, and algebraic constructions arising from affine geometry.

Current Work

Polygonal Number Sequences over Finite Fields

Investigating polynomial and geometric constructions that realize polygonal-number-type sequences through rational point counts over finite fields.

Zariski Cancellation Problem

Studying affine algebraic constructions related to cancellation phenomena and possible new families of counterexamples.

Mathieu-Zhao Subspaces

Exploring algebraic questions concerning Mathieu-Zhao subspaces and related structures.
