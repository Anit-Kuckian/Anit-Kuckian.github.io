About

A little about me

My work sits broadly at the intersection of algebra, finite fields, and affine algebraic geometry.

Mathematics researcher at IIT Gandhinagar

I am currently a Sabarmati Bridge Fellow at the Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar, where I continue working on problems in algebra and finite-field geometry.

My recent research involves rational points over finite fields, Danielewski and Double Danielewski varieties, and questions related to the Zariski Cancellation Problem. I am also increasingly interested in finite fields and algebraic coding theory.

Research

Research interests

My interests are broad within algebra, but the following areas currently form the center of my mathematical work.

Finite Fields

Polynomial arithmetic, factorization, primitive elements, finite-field constructions, and applications.

Algebraic Coding Theory

Codes over finite fields, algebraic constructions, covering problems, and structural coding questions.

Commutative Algebra

Affine domains, polynomial rings, exponential maps, filtrations, and algebraic invariants.

Affine Algebraic Geometry

Danielewski varieties, Makar-Limanov invariants, and the Zariski Cancellation Problem.

Rational Points

Counting rational points on algebraic varieties over finite fields.

Computational Algebra

SageMath, Macaulay2, Python, and experimental calculations supporting algebraic research.
