About

Mathematics through examples, structure, and algebra.

A short introduction to my background and mathematical interests.

I am interested in understanding algebraic structures through explicit examples, computations, and the patterns that emerge from them.

I am currently a Sabarmati Bridge Fellow at the Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar, where I continue working on problems in algebra and finite-field geometry.

My recent work involves rational points over finite fields, Danielewski-type varieties, and questions related to the Zariski Cancellation Problem. I am also developing my research in finite fields and algebraic coding theory.

Research

Research interests

The areas that currently define my mathematical interests.

AREA 01

Finite Fields

Polynomial arithmetic, irreducibility, primitive elements, cyclotomic structures, and applications.

AREA 02

Algebraic Coding Theory

Algebraic codes, finite-field methods, covering problems, and structural questions in coding theory.

AREA 03

Commutative Algebra

Affine domains, polynomial rings, filtrations, exponential maps, and algebraic invariants.

AREA 04

Affine Algebraic Geometry

Danielewski-type varieties, Makar-Limanov invariants, and cancellation phenomena.

AREA 05

Rational Points

Rational point counting for algebraic varieties defined over finite fields.

AREA 06

Computational Algebra

Experimental and symbolic computation using SageMath, Macaulay2, and Python.
