About

I am currently a Sabarmati Bridge Fellow in Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar.

My research interests lie broadly in algebra, finite fields, and their applications.

I am particularly interested in the interaction between finite fields, algebraic coding theory, commutative algebra, and affine algebraic geometry.

Main research interests

  • Finite Fields
  • Algebraic Coding Theory
  • Commutative Algebra
  • Affine Algebraic Geometry
  • Rational Points over Finite Fields

Research

My current research involves problems concerning rational points over finite fields and algebraic constructions associated with affine varieties.

I am also interested in algebraic coding theory and in understanding how methods from finite fields, algebra and geometry can be applied to problems arising in coding theory.

Other topics I am currently exploring include polygonal number sequences over finite fields, the Zariski Cancellation Problem, and Mathieu-Zhao subspaces.
