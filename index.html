About

Algebra, finite fields, and geometry.

I am currently a Sabarmati Bridge Fellow at the Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar. My research interests center on algebraic structures over finite fields and their interactions with coding theory and affine algebraic geometry.

My recent work includes rational point counting on Danielewski-type varieties and questions related to the Zariski Cancellation Problem. I am also exploring polynomial constructions over finite fields and algebraic coding theory.
