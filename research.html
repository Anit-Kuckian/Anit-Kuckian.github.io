Research

Research interests

My interests lie broadly in algebra, with current emphasis on finite fields and their applications, algebraic coding theory, and problems in commutative and affine algebra.

01

Finite Fields

Polynomial constructions, arithmetic phenomena, and rational-point questions over finite fields.

02

Algebraic Coding Theory

Algebraic structure in coding theory, with particular interest in finite-field and geometric methods.

03

Commutative Algebra

Affine algebras, exponential maps, locally nilpotent phenomena, and cancellation-type problems.

04

Affine Algebraic Geometry

Danielewski-type varieties, Makar-Limanov invariants, and the Zariski Cancellation Problem.

Current directions

Polygonal-number sequences over finite fields

Constructing polynomial systems whose rational-point counts realize structured numerical sequences.

Zariski Cancellation Problem

Studying affine-algebra constructions related to cancellation and possible new counterexample families.

Mathieu-Zhao subspaces

Exploring structural algebraic questions involving Mathieu-Zhao subspaces.
